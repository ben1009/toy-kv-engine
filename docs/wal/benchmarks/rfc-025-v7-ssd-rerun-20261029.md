# RFC 025 admitted-ticket grouping: requested SSD rerun

The admitted-ticket producer-framing candidate is **not qualified for retention**. The 1-, 8-, and 16-writer batch64 screens all had fewer than two eligible runs in at least one arm after the predeclared storage-timing filter. The 16-writer candidate's best eligible sample was faster and had lower p99 than the retained-v7 sample, but there was only one eligible sample in each of those arms. The point-write guards were skipped as specified by the plan. Production source remains at the retained baseline; the candidate patch is preserved in the [previous report](rfc-025-v7-admitted-group-20261029.md).

Before this comparison, four direct-I/O probe passes all failed the frozen SSD stability criteria. The user nevertheless requested the comparison on this drive, with stalls flagged and data preserved. This is an exploratory rerun, not evidence that the SSD was stable. The [preflight report](artifacts/rfc-025-v7-ssd-rerun-20261029-preflight-probe-summary.md) contains the full probe and explains why means can miss individual slow writes. No recognized competing build or benchmark appeared in the sampled host processes, but that does not rule out other interference or storage stalls.

## Method and limits

The test used the Solidigm SSDPFKNU010TZ on ext4 (`/dev/nvme0n1p3`), 750,000 puts, 1,024-byte values, batch size 64, no compaction, WAL Parallel, and latency sampling every tenth call. Each cell had one warmup and three scored runs per arm, a 120-second initial cooldown, and a 15-second inter-run gap. Arm order rotated according to the [fixed matrix](artifacts/rfc-025-v7-ssd-rerun-20261029-matrix.json); no score-selected replacement runs were added. The retained binary was used for both v4 and v7; a separate preserved binary ran the candidate.

The predeclared per-run filter flags mean FRONTIER write time of at least 0.12 ms or mean WAL `fdatasync` time of at least 0.6 ms. v4 has no FRONTIER metric. Neither mean reliably detects short DATA-write stalls; in particular an unflagged v4 sample is not proof of a clean storage path. A flagged run remains visible below and in the raw artifacts. The filter identifies suspicious timings; it does not prove the SSD was the only cause. The candidate must meet the batch throughput and p99 checks with at least two eligible samples in every arm before the planned point guards can run. Those conditions were not met.
