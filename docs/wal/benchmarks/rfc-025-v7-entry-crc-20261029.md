# RFC 025: contiguous v7 entry CRC experiment, 2026-10-29

**Rejected: the eight-writer best-score throughput guard fails. The retained Rust source and canonical executable are restored.**

[Draft PR #527](https://github.com/ben1009/toy-kv-engine/pull/527) contains the earlier retained optimizations. This continuation tests a smaller change suggested by the [CPU attribution](rfc-025-v7-batch-attribution-20261029.md): encode canonical entries, then checksum the contiguous encoded stream once instead of updating CRC for each entry header, field length, key, and value. V5/v6 keep their streaming checksum callback. Wire bytes, logical SHA256, admission charges, framing, scheduling, and sync-before-publication ordering are preserved.

## Best scored results

The ranking uses the highest-throughput eligible scored invocation for each exact executable and workload. P99 and CPU come from that same winning invocation. Warmups are excluded; raw maxima and every excluded run remain in the summary.

| Writers, batch64 | Retained v7 puts/s | Candidate puts/s | Throughput | Retained / candidate p99 ms | CPU |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1 | 23,204 | 38,488 | +65.9% | 13.919 / 10.077 | -7.1% |
| 8 | 418,862 | 376,372 | -10.1% | 3.148 / 3.140 | +15.2% |

Both v7 arms have three eligible scored runs in each cell. The eight-writer throughput loss rejects the candidate despite its slightly better p99. The four conditional guards (point writes with 1/8/16 writers and batch64 with 16 writers) are **not run**, as prescribed by the frozen plan. CPU is diagnostic and does not veto retention.

The solo gain is heavily confounded by storage timing. Its winning runs both use 11,719 syncs, but total fdatasync time is 17.00 s for retained v7 and 8.68 s for the candidate. The retained winner also spends 1.19 s writing FRONTIERs, versus 0.70 s for the candidate. These counters overlap other work; they do not form an additive wall-time decomposition. Solo CPU falls 7.1% between the winners. At eight writers it rises 15.2%; sync count changes from 1,604 to 1,703 and total recorded coalescing wait from 717 to 826 ms. The experiment does not isolate causality for those scheduling changes.

## Protocol and validation

The [frozen plan](artifacts/rfc-025-v7-entry-crc-20261029-plan.json) and [matrix](artifacts/rfc-025-v7-entry-crc-20261029-matrix.json) specify one warmup per arm followed by three alternating triplets: retained v4 control, retained v7, and candidate v7. Both screen cells finish regardless of performance. This produces **24 completed invocations: 18 scored and 6 warmups**, with no score-selected replacement runs. All use fresh databases under the local repo, 750,000 puts, batch64, 1-KiB values, a 2-GiB SST target, and latency sampling every 10 batch calls. Each cell starts with 60 seconds of cooldown and uses 15-second gaps.

The host is the same Core i9-13900T and Solidigm SSD on local ext4. A one-second process audit detects no recognized competing build, test, profiling, or benchmark process. No source changes, tests, builds, or profiles overlap capture. Disposable databases are removed. The predefined mask independently excludes a run when mean FRONTIER write is at least 1 ms or mean fdatasync is at least 1.5 ms. Two eight-writer v4 scores are flagged. None of the v7 scores are flagged, but the solo sync totals and high p99 show why falling below an aggregate threshold does not establish stall-free conditions.

The candidate passes `cargo make check` with **1,974 tests**, including a new comparison against the independent v5 encoder across CRC/data-block boundaries and untouched destination padding. Nineteen focused canonical/codec tests pass with retries disabled. Release build and direct spelling verification pass before capture. After rejection, the single Rust file is restored with a fresh modification time and the canonical executable is replaced through a fresh inode, preserving hardlinked saved binaries. Final restored checks pass **1,973 tests** with no retries or skips, plus direct spelling verification. One leveled-compaction test passes after 32.6 seconds and is reported as slow.

The original visual diff is preserved compressed, and an [applicable source patch](artifacts/rfc-025-v7-entry-crc-20261029-source.patch) reconstructs the candidate from `00d65d82`.
